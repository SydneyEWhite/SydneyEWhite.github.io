Research interests

My current research is mainly in ecological statistics, but I'm, in general, passionate about applying statistcs to a worthy cause -- whether that be ecology, social equality, or political legislation. In 2023, I began working part-time with a computational land management research group where I became lead author of my first paper and later co-authored another paper. In 2025-2026, I conducted research utilizing neural networks to predict attributes of users based on the movement of their computer mouse. I recently started work at the University of Georgia's Warnell School of Forestry and Natural Resources, where I handle statistical analyses and modeling for data collected by Inventory & Monitoring Division of the National Park Service.
