About

I hold a B.S. in Industrial Engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology (2019) and an M.Sc. in Statistics from Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (2026), where my thesis examined approaches for handling multi-temporal data in Hidden Markov Models, with applications to animal movement

My current research is mainly in ecological statistics, but I'm, in general, passionate about applying statistcs to a worthy cause -- whether that be ecology, social equality, or political legislation. In 2023, I began working part-time with a computational land management research group where I became lead author of my first paper and later co-authored another paper. In 2025-2026, I conducted research utilizing neural networks to predict attributes of users based on the movement of their computer mouse. I recently started work at the University of Georgia's Warnell School of Forestry and Natural Resources, where I handle statistical analyses and modeling for data collected by Inventory & Monitoring Division of the National Park Service.

Along with my academic pursuits, I worked for more than 5 years at Wayfair -- first in the US then in the Berlin office. Throughout my time, I focused on analytical problems in different departments before eventually heading Wayfair's B2B Marketing Operations in Europe.

I have experience with applied statistics (e.g. causal inference, time series modeling, Bayesian methods, ML), data science, data analytics, data visualization, and financial reporting. My software experience includes Python (and Jupyter Notebooks), R, SQL (Vertica, MySQL, GBQ), Looker, Tableau, JavaScript, React Native, HTML, and Alteryx.
